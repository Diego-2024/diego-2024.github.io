About me

I am a master's student at Guangzhou University. My research focuses on video prediction and temporal representation learning.

I am interested in how learned representations can capture temporal structure and support future-frame prediction. My work explores hierarchical predictive models, motion and appearance modeling, and autoregressive video forecasting.
